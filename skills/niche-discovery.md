# Skill: Niche / Multi-Bagger Discovery

How to hunt under-followed names that could blow up (the SanDisk/Hynix-on-AI-memory
pattern), responsibly.

1. Start from a STRUCTURAL driver, not a tip: a confirmed secular trend (AI compute,
   electrification, re-shoring, nuclear, GLP-1, defense) with durable demand.
2. Walk the value chain to the LESS-obvious beneficiary: who supplies the supplier?
   who has pricing power / capacity others need? (use CrossMarketConnector logic).
3. Screen for: small/mid cap, accelerating revenue, expanding margins, insider/13F or
   smart-money interest, low analyst coverage. Use `propose_ticker` to add candidates.
4. Verify it's real: `sec_financials`, filings, multiple independent sources. A niche
   name needs MORE corroboration, not less — small caps are where fraud/hype live.
5. Size SMALL and basket it: many small asymmetric bets, not one concentrated gamble.
   Define the catalyst and the invalidation up front.
6. Honestly flag illiquidity, dilution risk, and that most niche bets fail — the
   winners pay for the losers only if each is sized to survive being wrong.
