# Skill: Evidence Verification

How to turn a lead into corroborated evidence that can clear the house-rules bar.

1. Treat every web/news/social/memory item as an UNTRUSTED lead until corroborated.
2. Corroborate: find >= 2 INDEPENDENT credible sources that agree. Independent =
   different outlets/origins, not the same wire reprinted. Note each as a citation.
3. Seek a PRIMARY source where it matters: SEC filing (`sec_financials`/`recent_filings`),
   official company release, exchange/regulator data. Primary is required for large or
   concentrated positions; corroborated secondary justifies a small starter.
4. Date-check everything against the current authoritative time — stale facts mislead.
5. Record citations on your report (source, claim, verified, primary). Uncited claims
   will be (correctly) vetoed by the RiskAgent.
6. If you cannot corroborate, say so and mark UNVERIFIED — it can lower confidence but
   must not raise it. Honesty over a forced thesis.
