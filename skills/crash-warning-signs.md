# Skill: Crash / Selloff Warning Signs

How to watch for regime shifts and de-risk EARLY (nobody predicts crashes precisely —
the goal is to read rising risk and cut exposure before it's forced).

Watch for clustering of:
- Volatility regime flip (use QuantAgent regime + rising realized/implied vol, VIX spikes).
- Credit stress: widening high-yield spreads (HYG/JNK weakness vs Treasuries).
- Breadth deterioration: index holding up while most stocks fall (narrow leadership).
- Rates/FX shocks: rapid yield moves, yen-carry unwind, dollar spikes, curve changes.
- Liquidity/funding stress, big forced-seller headlines, leverage unwinds.
- Macro: policy surprises, recession signals, geopolitical shocks.

Actions (the RiskGovernor automates the hard tiers, but reason ahead of it):
- Rising risk -> trim high-beta/illiquid, raise cash, favor quality/defensives, add hedges.
- Don't try to call the exact top; reduce gross exposure as evidence of stress accumulates.
- A confirmed risk-off regime = protect capital first; opportunities come after.
