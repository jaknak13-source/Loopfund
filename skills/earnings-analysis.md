# Skill: Earnings Analysis & Timing

How to read an earnings situation and time around it.

1. BEFORE earnings: do NOT add to a position right before its report if the outcome
   is uncertain — the binary risk isn't worth it. Note the date; wait or size tiny.
2. Pull `sec_financials` + recent `recent_filings` (10-Q/8-K) and news for the name.
3. On the report, judge: revenue vs trend, margin direction, FORWARD GUIDANCE (often
   matters more than the print), and analyst-revision direction.
4. Reaction > result: a stock that falls on a "good" print (sell-the-news) or rises
   on a "bad" one tells you what was already priced. Trade the reaction, not the headline.
5. Post-earnings drift: strong beat + raised guidance + positive reaction often keeps
   running for weeks — a tradeable window.
6. Always corroborate the numbers with the primary filing (SEC), never just headlines.
7. Flag the next earnings date for every holding so the risk agent can account for it.
