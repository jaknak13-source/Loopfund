# Skill: Cross-Market Impact Chains

How to trace a shock in one market into a profit somewhere else (second/third-order).

1. Identify the SHOCK or trend (demand surge, shortage, policy, weather, rate move).
2. Walk the chain link by link: shock -> who is directly affected -> their inputs /
   suppliers / substitutes -> the downstream sector that ultimately re-prices.
   Examples:
   - AI datacenter capex -> GPU demand -> HBM/NAND memory -> MU / WDC / SK Hynix.
   - Energy shift to nuclear -> uranium demand -> miners (URA/URNM) + SMR builders (OKLO/SMR).
   - Drought/La Nina -> crop yields -> grain prices -> fertilizer -> ag equipment.
   - Chip shortage -> vehicle supply -> used-car & rental pricing.
3. Find the END-OF-CHAIN beneficiary the crowd hasn't priced yet (often smaller/indirect).
4. For EACH link, state confidence + what would confirm or break it. A chain is only as
   strong as its weakest link — long speculative chains are low confidence.
5. Output concrete tickers + the chain reasoning; hand to analysts to verify each link.
6. Never present a chain as proven — it's a hypothesis to test, sized small until confirmed.
