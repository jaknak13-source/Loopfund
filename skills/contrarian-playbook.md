# Skill: Contrarian Playbook

How to find overlooked or contrarian setups and judge when consensus is actually wrong —
with evidence, not contrarianism for its own sake.

1. State the CONSENSUS in one sentence: what does the crowd believe and why? If you can't
   articulate the other side fairly, you don't understand the trade. Read the bear/bull case
   via `web_research`, `rss_headlines`, `reddit_hot`, `stocktwits_symbol`.
2. Find the DISCONFIRMING evidence: what fact, visible in the data, does the consensus ignore
   or misweight? Anchor on primary numbers — `sec_financials` / `fundamentals` (margins,
   cash, debt maturities), `estimate_fair_value` (price-implied expectations).
3. Use the reverse-DCF as your edge gauge: if `price_implied_growth` bakes in collapse for a
   healthy business (or heroic growth for a fading one), the crowd is mispricing — that gap is
   the setup (valuation-playbook).
4. Check positioning and capitulation: `insider_transactions` / `congress_trades` (insiders
   buying their own beaten name is a strong tell); sentiment washed-out vs euphoric. Smart
   money acting against the crowd corroborates; against you, reconsider.
5. Separate CHEAP from BROKEN: a value trap is cheap because it's deteriorating. Demand a
   reason the market is wrong (overreaction, forced selling, misread accounting, temporary
   shock) — not just a low multiple. No catalyst -> no trade.
6. Require >= 2 independent credible sources, primary for the load-bearing fact
   (evidence-verification). The more it disagrees with consensus, the MORE corroboration it needs.
7. Size for the chance you're wrong: start small, define the invalidation up front, add only
   as the thesis confirms. Being early and being wrong look identical for a while.

PITFALLS
- Contrarian for its own sake: the crowd is usually right. Disagreeing is not an edge unless
  you hold a specific fact they're misweighting.
- Value trap: confusing a falling knife with a bargain. Check whether fundamentals are
  stabilizing or still deteriorating before calling a bottom.
- Catalyst-less cheap: undervaluation alone can persist for years. Identify what forces the
  re-rate and roughly when, or accept NO_ACTION.
- Crowded "contrarian" trade: when everyone claims to be contrarian, you're consensus. Verify
  positioning with flows/sentiment, not your own assumption that you're alone.
- Deterministic evidence over conviction: the gap must show up in the numbers
  (`estimate_fair_value`, `sec_financials`), not just in how strongly you disagree. Dry-mode, EUR base.
